How it works

A shared wish list for the family. Everyone keeps their own list, and you can mark things you're buying for each other so nobody buys the same present twice. The person whose list it is never finds out what's been bought, until they choose to open their presents.

1. Sign in 2. Add things to your list 3. Buy something for someone 4. My shopping 5. Add to someone else's list 6. After the birthday or Christmas Keeping it a surprise Questions

1. Sign in

  1. Type your first name and the family code (ask whoever sent you the link).
  2. Always use the same name, spelled the same way. Your name is your list: "Sam" and "Sammy" would be two different lists. Capital letters don't matter.
  3. Your phone or computer remembers you, so you only do this once on each device.

2. Add things to your list

  1. Tap My list.
  2. Find the thing you want in a shop's website, copy its link, and paste it into the box. The name, price and picture usually fill in by themselves after a second or two.
  3. Check the name and price, and add any notes (size, colour, "any of these", "second-hand is fine").
  4. Tick ★ Really want this for favourites (they go to the top), and set How many? if you'd like more than one, e.g. 2 mugs.
  5. Tap Add to my list.

No link? Just type what it is ("Bath bombs", "Book tokens") and add it.

On a phone with the Amazon app: tap Share → Copy on the product, then paste the whole message into the box. The page picks out the link and the product name for you.

To change something, tap Edit. To take it off, tap Remove.

3. Buy something for someone

  1. Tap the person's name along the top to see their list. (A name appears once there's something on their list.) Things with a ★ are the ones they really want.
  2. Tap the item's name to open the shop.
  3. When you decide to get it, tap I'll get this. It changes to You're getting this, and everyone else sees Your name is getting this, so they won't buy it too.
  4. Changed your mind? Tap undo to free it up again. Only you can undo your own.
  5. If they want more than one (it says "wants 2"), the button says I'll get one, so several people can each get one. "1 of 2 taken" shows how many are spoken for, and +1 lets you get another yourself while there are still some left.

4. My shopping

The My shopping tab lists everything you've said you'll get, for everyone. Tick Bought once you've actually bought it. The red number shows how many are still to buy.

If someone removes an item you'd said you'd get, it stays here marked Removed from the list by …, so you know (and can decide whether to keep or return it). Tap Take off my shopping once you've dealt with it.

5. Add to someone else's list

Secret suggestions. Open someone's list and tap + Add something to …'s list. "Secret suggestion" is ticked to start with: everyone except that person can see it (e.g. "Mum would love this"), marked Secret suggestion from you. Untick it if they should see it too.

Lists for people who won't use this, like young children or grandparents: tap + Someone else, type their name, and add things for them. Anyone can then buy from it as normal. If they do sign in one day with that name, they'll find their list waiting.

Whoever added an item can edit or remove it, as can the person whose list it's on (except secret ones, which they can't see at all).

6. After the birthday or Christmas

On your own list, tap 🎁 Open my presents. Everything that was bought moves off your list into Presents received, showing who each one was from. Things nobody bought stay on your list. Only do this after the occasion, because it shows you who's getting what.

For lists of people who don't use this (like Grandpa), anyone can tap Tidy up …'s list afterwards instead.

Keeping it a surprise

On your own list you'll never see what's been bought, who's getting it, or any secret suggestions, until you tap Open my presents. Everyone else can see all of it, so they can avoid buying the same thing twice.

It works on trust. The family code keeps strangers out, but nothing checks names, so please only sign in as yourself.

Questions

The name or picture didn't fill in

Some shops (especially Amazon) block automatic look-ups. Just type the name and price yourself. The link still works when people tap it. A picture is nice but not needed.

I don't see changes someone else just made

The page catches up whenever you switch tabs or come back to it. You can also tap Refresh at the top.

I signed in with the wrong name, or I'm on a new phone

Tap not you? under the title and sign in again with your usual name. Your list is still there. It's saved online, not on your phone.

Someone has two lists because of a spelling mistake

Sign in with the spelling you want to keep and re-add the items, then remove the old ones under the other spelling. Or ask whoever set this up to fix the name in the spreadsheet.

I bought something but forgot to mark it

Open their list and tap I'll get this now, then tick Bought in My shopping. It doesn't matter when you do it.

What if I remove something that someone has already bought?

You won't be told, so the surprise is safe. The person getting it still sees it in their My shopping tab, marked as removed, so they know.

Something on my list says "added by …"

Someone added it to your list for you (not as a secret). You can edit or remove it like anything else.

I tapped "Open my presents" too early

The page can't undo it, and you'll have seen who's getting what. Whoever runs the spreadsheet can put things back by clearing the dates in the archived columns. Please act surprised anyway!

Where is everything kept?

In a private Google spreadsheet belonging to whoever set this up. Only people with the family code can see or change the lists through this page.
