How it works

A shared wish list for the family. Everyone keeps their own list, and you can mark things you're buying for each other so nobody buys the same present twice. The person whose list it is never finds out what's been bought.

1. Sign in

  1. Type your first name and the family code (ask whoever sent you the link).
  2. Always use the same name, spelled the same way. Your name is your list: "Sam" and "Sammy" would be two different lists. Capital letters don't matter.
  3. Your phone or computer remembers you, so you only do this once on each device.

2. Add things to your list

  1. Tap My list.
  2. Find the thing you want in a shop's website, copy its link, and paste it into the box. The name, price and picture usually fill in by themselves after a second or two.
  3. Check the name and price, and add any notes (size, colour, "any of these", "second-hand is fine").
  4. Tap Add to my list.

No link? Just type what it is ("Bath bombs", "Book tokens") and add it.

On a phone with the Amazon app: tap Share → Copy on the product, then paste the whole message into the box. The page picks out the link and the product name for you.

To take something off, tap Remove.

3. Buy something for someone

  1. Tap the person's name along the top to see their list. (A name only appears once that person has added something.)
  2. Tap the item's name to open the shop.
  3. When you decide to get it, tap I'll get this. It changes to You're getting this, and everyone else sees Bought by your name, so they won't buy it too.
  4. Changed your mind? Tap undo to free it up again. Only you can undo your own.

Keeping it a surprise

On your own list you'll never see what's been bought or who bought it. Everyone else can see it on your list, so they can avoid buying the same thing twice.

It works on trust. The family code keeps strangers out, but nothing checks names, so please only sign in as yourself.

Questions

The name or picture didn't fill in

Some shops (especially Amazon) block automatic look-ups. Just type the name and price yourself. The link still works when people tap it. A picture is nice but not needed.

I don't see changes someone else just made

Tap Refresh at the top. The page also refreshes when you come back to it.

I signed in with the wrong name, or I'm on a new phone

Tap not you? under the title and sign in again with your usual name. Your list is still there. It's saved online, not on your phone.

Someone has two lists because of a spelling mistake

Sign in with the spelling you want to keep and re-add the items, then remove the old ones under the other spelling. Or ask whoever set this up to fix the name in the spreadsheet.

I bought something but forgot to mark it

Open their list and tap I'll get this now. It doesn't matter when you do it.

What if I remove something that someone has already bought?

It disappears for everyone, including whoever was getting it, and you won't be told it was bought. It's best to leave things on your list until after the birthday or holiday.

Where is everything kept?

In a private Google spreadsheet belonging to whoever set this up. Only people with the family code can see or change the lists through this page.
